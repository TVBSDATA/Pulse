Short-Drama Survey Insight

Plenty of readers binge short dramas. Only 9% have paid.

Of 1,169 responses, 74% have watched short dramas and 41% say "just one more episode" is a lie. But 82% spent nothing in the past month. These viewers want free and fewer ads. Revenue comes from advertising and content, not subscriptions.

02 What they think

Platforms

Hongguo is home for heavy viewers; YouTube is the front door

49% watch most on YouTube and 30% on Hongguo. Among Free Binge-Watchers, Hongguo reaches 54%. People get hooked on YouTube (43%) and Facebook (41%); app stores only 2%.

Paying

The path to payment is narrow

56% want every episode free or nearly free; 36% want fewer ads. Even among Payers, 39% chose free or very cheap.

AI

Most don't mind AI

73% would watch (including "lacks human touch"); 22% swipe away once they know it's AI. Those are concentrated among Bystanders: 56%.

03 How to use these numbers

Each card has three layers: what the survey says, what to try first, and what it didn't ask.

Genres

Revenge and CEO romance lead

Survey says

Revenge & comeback 39%, urban romance 36%, light comedy 26%, fantasy 23%. 17% want less formulaic plots.

Try first

A batch of each top genre, then one comedy or fantasy title to test the difference. Articles can break down the story formulas.

Not yet known

Episode length, episode count, specific titles.

Paying

Free is the entry fee; ads must be light

Survey says

82% spent NT$0 last month; 56% want free or nearly free; 36% want fewer ads; 17% say they wouldn't use a drama app.

Try first

Free viewing first, ads starting light, one low-interruption brand at a time. Paid tiers come later.

Not yet known

Willingness to pay, tolerable ad length, what viewers do after an ad.

Entry points

Social feeds, not app stores

Survey says

Hooked on: YouTube 43%, Facebook 41%, Douyin 15%; app stores 2%. Most-used: YouTube 49%, Hongguo 30%, ReelShort 8%.

Try first

Post trailers and highlights on YouTube and Facebook and measure the click-back rate.

Not yet known

Conversion rate and acquisition cost per platform.

AI

Most don't care if it's AI

Survey says

73% accept AI dramas, 5% can't tell, 22% swipe away once they know.

Try first

AI content is fine if clearly labeled. A quick "AI or human?" quiz with survey numbers on the result page.

Not yet known

How AI labels affect viewing and brand perception.

Segments

Heavy viewers and the small paying group

Survey says

41% can't stop; 102 people paid, 15 of them over NT$1,000. 166 respondents are 60+, and 88% have watched.

Try first

The paying group is small; interview it separately. For older viewers, try family-drama titles shared through LINE and Facebook.

Not yet known

What the payers buy, where, and how much.

Curation

Some want someone to pick for them

Survey says

10% want curated picks; Taiwanese cast and stories 2%; natural Taiwanese-style subtitles 6%.

Try first

A "this week's picks" entry point. Treat localization as a bonus, not the main pitch.

Not yet known

Whether curation lifts repeat visits; the value of local production.
